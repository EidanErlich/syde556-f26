\question{1}{4}
A rectified-linear neuron fires at rate $a = G[J] = \max(J, 0)$, with input current $J = \alpha\langle x, e\rangle + J^{\mathrm{bias}}$. Consider a one-dimensional neuron with encoder $e = +1$. Derive expressions for the gain $\alpha$ and the bias $J^{\mathrm{bias}}$ so that the neuron has maximum firing rate $a^{\max}$ at $x = 1$ and an $x$-intercept at $x = \xi$ (the value of $x$ at which it begins to fire). Show your work.
\vspace{4.2cm}

\question{3}{4}
A population of $N$ neurons represents a scalar value $x$. The input is sampled at $M$ values, giving the population activity matrix $\mathbf{A}$.

(a) Write the matrix equation for the optimal decoders $\mathbf d$ that reconstruct $x$ from $\mathbf A$, \emph{including} the term that accounts for neural noise of variance $\sigma^2$.
\vspace{2.2cm}

(b) Now suppose the population represents a 2-D vector $\mathbf x$ (with $N = 10$ neurons) and at some moment has produced the activity vector $\mathbf a$. How do you calculate the decoded estimate $\hat{\mathbf x}$? Write the equation, and draw each vector and matrix in it as a labelled rectangle with its dimensions.
\answerbox{3.0cm}

(c) In one or two sentences, explain why including the noise term changes how the decoders behave when the activities are noisy.
\vspace{2.0cm}
